Attachment A: Statement of Requirement

Background
The Smarter Data Program (SDP) within the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) is progressing the adoption of
artificial intelligence (AI) and generative AI capabilities to support productivity, decision-making, service delivery
and workforce capability uplift across the organisation.

The ATO has an established history of using data, analytics and machine learning to improve operational
effectiveness and decision-making. More recently, Microsoft Copilot has created opportunities to improve staff
productivity through generative AI while maintaining appropriate governance, security and oversight.

The ATO has identified priority AI use cases and is seeking external specialist support to develop a fit for
purpose Microsoft Copilot Prompt Library. The objective is to enable staff to consistently and effectively use
Microsoft Copilot through reusable prompt assets, structured prompting methodologies, user guidance and
governance arrangements.

The engagement will support the development of reusable AI assets that can be adopted across business areas
and, where appropriate, extended into Microsoft Copilot Skills and other future Copilot capabilities.

Business requirements
To support the ATO's AI enablement objectives, the ATO is seeking a supplier with demonstrated expertise in:
- Microsoft 365 Copilot
- Prompt engineering and optimisation
- Design and implementation of reusable Copilot Skills assets
- Generative AI adoption and people capability uplift
- Governance and assurance frameworks
- Knowledge transfer and organisational adoption
- Embedding prompt libraries into Microsoft workflows

The supplier must provide services that establish a fit for purpose, reusable and sustainable Prompt Library
capability within the Microsoft 365 Copilot environment, including consideration of future implementation through
Copilot Skills where appropriate.

ATO's Smarter Data Program requires:

Prompt Development and Testing
The supplier must support the development and testing of prompts for priority business activities aligned to ATO
priority AI use cases. Prompts must be designed for practical use within Microsoft 365 Copilot environments.
This includes:
- Development of 20-30 production-ready prompts
- Coverage of approximately 10-15 priority business tasks
- Testing and refinement of prompts
- Sample outputs and expected outcomes
- User input parameters and configurable variables
- Exception handling guidance where output quality may vary
- Practical recommendations for achieving greater output consistency
- Identification of prompts that are suitable for future implementation as Copilot Skills

Prompt Design and Governance
The supplier must develop a standardised prompt engineering framework that supports consistent prompt
design and execution across priority business activities.
This includes:
- Standard prompt architecture and templates
- Prompt engineering methodology
- Prompt design principles and guidance
- Prompt quality assurance approach
- Documentation of limitations, assumptions and usage considerations
- Reusable prompt patterns that can be applied across multiple business scenarios
- Guidance on designing prompts that can be extended into Copilot Skills

Prompt Library Design
The supplier must design a Prompt Library capable of being implemented within the ATO's Microsoft 365
environment.
This includes:
- Library structure and organisation
- Taxonomy and metadata design
- Search and navigation approach
- Reusable prompt categorisation model
- Consideration of future scalability and maintainability
- Categorisation of prompts that may be suitable for future Copilot Skills implementation

Governance and Sustainability
The supplier must provide governance recommendations that support ongoing maintenance and scalability.
This includes:
- Ownership and accountability model
- Prompt review and approval processes
- Version control approach
- Quality assurance processes
- Governance responsibilities
- Maintenance and lifecycle management recommendations
- Governance considerations for Copilot Skills, including ownership, maintenance and approval processes

Knowledge Transfer and Capability Uplift
The supplier must demonstrate a structured approach to capability transfer and organisational adoption.
This includes:
- User guidance and instruction materials
- Reusable templates and artefacts
- Recommendations to support sustainable capability development across business areas
- Guidance materials for maintaining prompts and converting suitable prompts into Copilot Skills

Security, Confidentiality and Information Management
Respondents must demonstrate:
- Secure handling of ATO information
- Compliance with Australian Government security and privacy expectations
- Clear arrangements regarding data storage, access, and retention
- Ability to work with sensitive or protected information where required.

Intellectual Property and Reuse
The ATO will have sole ownership and reuse rights to all prompts, prompt libraries, Copilot Skills, supporting
artefacts, documentation and deliverables developed through this engagement, subject to the terms of the Deed
and Work Order.

Deliverables
The supplier must provide the following deliverables.
- Copilot Prompt Library
- Copilot Prompt Development Framework and Methodology
- Prompt Testing, Assumptions, Limitations and Exception Report
- User Guides and Usage Instructions
- Risk Assessment and Mitigation Recommendations
- Copilot Skills Readiness Guidance, including recommendations on which prompts are suitable for
  implementation as Copilot Skills and considerations for future adoption
